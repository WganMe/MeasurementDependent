## Where the submission now goes

The PeerJ submission is generated by `convert_to_peerj.py` into the sibling `PeerJ`
directory (`peerj-main.tex`, `peerj-si.tex`, `peerj-cover.tex`). The sources
(`main.tex`, `main_si.tex`) and their class are the source of truth the PeerJ build
converts from.

## Data

The **Newcastle Urban Observatory** data analysed by this work are **not**
redistributed in this repository. They are published independently by Newcastle
University under CC BY 4.0, and the manuscript's Data Availability statement
points to that source. What is archived here is the derived census index
(`uo_archive_index.json`) and the fitted results — that is, our own work
*about* those data, not the data themselves.

Seven identifiers more than 60 km from the projection origin were excluded before
any spatial statistic was computed; see the manuscript's Limitations.
